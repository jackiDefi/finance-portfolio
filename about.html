I’m Togrul Mirzayev, a finance professional with 4+ years of experience across audit, financial reporting, and budgeting. I’m currently Lead Reporting & Budgeting Specialist at AzIntelecom, Azerbaijan’s national telecom and IT infrastructure operator.

This portfolio is the visible output of that combination. Five industry deep-dives, each starting from first principles, instrumented as a live model rather than a static PDF. The goal isn’t to demonstrate Excel proficiency; it’s to show that I understand why the numbers in those industries move.
